Automation & AI

Systems I designed and built myself with Google Ads Scripts, the Google Ads API, Apps Script, Vertex AI and Claude Code. They run at MCC level across accounts.

Flagship system · PMax & Search

Automated ad asset optimisation

Three connected scripts: analyse → AI suggests → apply

1 · Analyser

Pulls asset-level data from the Google Ads API: PMax asset groups, RSA headlines and descriptions, extensions. Scores each asset by CTR against its type average, conversions and ROAS against dynamic campaign targets. Video is scored on conversions and ROAS only. Output: prioritised recommendations in Google Sheets.

2 · AI generator

Gemini 2.5 Flash via Vertex AI writes three replacements for each weak asset. Only the ad text goes to the model, never metrics, budgets or business data. Character limits and duplicates are checked, then a human approves.

3 · Auto-replacer

Applies approved changes through the API. PMax: unlink old asset, create new, relink. Search: replaces text inside the existing RSA and keeps the ad ID. DRY RUN mode for safe checks.

104asset variants in the first full run
€0.08total AI cost of that run
20–30 mininstead of several hours of manual work

Campaign launch pipeline

Repeatable pipeline that builds and launches campaigns for any new country, with AI-generated ad copy in the local language. Every launch follows one tested setup instead of manual work.

Daily ROMI adjustments

Reads each campaign's previous-day ROMI and adjusts campaigns automatically, so spend follows return every day.

Target monitoring

Flags campaigns that drift from CPA, ROAS or cost-per-profile targets, so problems surface in days, not weeks.

Anomaly detection

Alerts on sudden jumps in CPA or spend, day over day and week over week, before they eat the budget.

PMax channel mix

Tracks where Performance Max spend goes (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display) and catches shifts to low-value inventory early.

PMax placement exclusions

Finds underperforming sites and apps in Performance Max and adds them to exclusion lists automatically.

Keywords & negatives

Automated campaign analysis: n-gram mining of search terms, new keyword themes and negative keyword lists.

Product mapping by performance & margin

Maps every product in the feed into tiers by its sales performance and margin, then routes each tier into campaigns with its own ROAS target. High-margin winners get more budget, weak items stop draining it, and the mapping updates itself as data changes.

Merchant Center monitoring

Watches disapproved products and feed errors and sends an alert, so products do not silently drop out of Shopping and PMax.

Broken link checker

Finds ads and sitelinks that lead to 404 or broken landing pages and pauses them automatically.

Email alerts & reports

Scripts send alerts and scheduled performance reports by email, so issues and results reach the team without opening the account.

Reporting & pacing

Cross-account MCC reports, budget pacing and Jira → Sheets integrations.
